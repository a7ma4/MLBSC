Cybersecurity Governance

Securing your digital assets and ensuring business continuity in a rapidly evolving threat landscape:

Governance, Risk
& Compliance (GRC)

  • Building Governance Frameworks: Designing and developing cybersecurity policies in line with national (e.g., NCA) and international standards (e.g., ISO 27001).
  • Compliance Measurement: Conducting regular assessments to verify compliance with legislative and regulatory requirements.
  • Cyber Risk Management: Identifying potential risks, analyzing their business impact, and implementing proactive remediation strategies.

Strategic Protection
& Assessment

  • Penetration Testing: Simulating real-world cyberattacks to uncover and remediate flaws in systems and applications before exploitation.
  • Vulnerability Assessment: Conducting scheduled ecosystem-wide scans to pinpoint technical weaknesses and exposures.
  • Cloud Security: Securing cloud migrations while guaranteeing data encryption mechanisms and robust identity access management.

Business Continuity
& Response

  • Disaster Recovery Plans (DRP): Formulating robust restoration strategies for systems and records to maintain zero operations downtime.
  • Incident Management: Establishing specialized response units and protocols for swift and efficient mitigation of security threats.
  • Security Awareness Training: Delivering structured learning modules to employees to foster a defensive corporate security culture.
